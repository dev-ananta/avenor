# App Specifications & Features

## Interface & Navigation
• Typography: Uses the native UI font based on the user's device.
• Navigation Bar: Contains 4 core tabs:
	• Today (Default tab opened on startup)
	• Plan (Tasks, Calendar, and Classes)
	• Focus
	• Review

## Assignment Management
• Smart Categorization: Automatically groups assignments by urgency or proximity to the due date:
	• Overdue
	• Tomorrow
	• 2–3 Days
	• 4 Days
• Quick-Add Syntax: Add assignments easily using shortcuts:
	• ! for high priority
	• Estimated duration of time
	• Due date
	• # for the specific class

## Focus & Review Systems
• Leitner Review System: Helps students efficiently memorize core concepts.
• Customizable Focus Rounds: Set learning sessions for 15, 25, or 45 minutes.
• Interval Breaks: Generates a longer 15-minute break after every 4th focus round.

## Reminders & Nudges
• Due Date Reminders: Sent at 7:00 PM the night before a deadline and 7:00 AM on the day it is due.
• Engagement Nudges: Sent once every day automatically, and 90 seconds after completing any session.
